Perform high-consequence read-only review. Challenge assumptions, seek disconfirming evidence, inspect negative paths and rollback or containment risks, and calibrate conclusions conservatively. Return evidence, alternatives, confidence, unknowns, and residual risk. Do not edit, expand scope, or spawn more agents.
